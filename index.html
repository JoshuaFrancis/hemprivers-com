Product streams

Three primary hemp product categories serving distinct end markets.

01

Fibre and hurd

Long-line bast fibre for technical textiles, geotextiles, and composite materials. Hurds (shiv) for hempcrete, animal bedding, and horticultural growing media. Processed at partner decortication facilities in Alberta and Ontario with traceability from field to finished bale.

02

CBD biomass and extract

Dried CBD-dominant floral biomass for extraction by nutraceutical and cosmetic manufacturers. Full-spectrum crude oil and broad-spectrum distillate available in drum quantities. All material sourced from AAFC-registered cultivators and tested for cannabinoid profile, heavy metals, pesticides, and mycotoxins.

03

Grain and seed

Dehulled hemp hearts, cold-pressed hemp seed oil, and whole seed for food manufacturing. Non-GMO, Canadian-origin with grain-grade certificates. Suitable for protein powder blending, plant-based food formulation, and organic bakery applications. Export documentation for US, EU, and UK markets included.
